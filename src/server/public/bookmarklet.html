Capture bookmarklet

Instagram's export contains everything you did, but nothing about who engaged with you — that data lives in other people's exports. This captures it from lists you open yourself.

📥 IG Capture Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

Using it

  1. Open one of your own posts on instagram.com and click its likes count (or the comments, or your story's viewer list).
  2. Scroll that list to the bottom yourself. The panel counts names as they appear.
  3. Check the type selector is right, then hit Save capture.
  4. The file lands in your downloads. If npm run watch is pointed at that folder it ingests on its own; otherwise move it into your watched folder.

Why this is safe

It makes zero network requests and never scrolls for you. It reads text your browser has already drawn because you scrolled. Instagram cannot distinguish it from you looking at the page, because that is all that happens. Tools that log in and enumerate lists in the background are a different thing entirely, and they are what gets accounts disabled.

Limits worth knowing

  • Comment text is best-effort — it is scraped from the rendered row and can pick up stray interface words.
  • Story viewers are only visible for 24 hours, so those have to be captured in the window.
  • Re-capturing the same post is harmless: rows are keyed on person + post, so nothing duplicates.
